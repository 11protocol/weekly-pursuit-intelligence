# 11Protocol Weekly Pursuit Intelligence

### Federal pursuit intelligence for GovCon capture and business development teams

Most federal procurement information tells you **what happened**.

The question for a capture or BD team is:

> **What should we be paying attention to right now — and why?**

11Protocol Weekly Pursuit Intelligence is an experimental weekly brief that uses federal procurement data to identify contracts and signals that may deserve attention, prioritize what appears most relevant, and translate those signals into potential pursuit or monitoring actions.

## What you'll find in the briefs

Depending on the sector, briefs may highlight:

* Potential recompetes and contract transitions
* Funding, growth, extension, or reduction signals
* Acquisition and contract risk
* Incumbent and competitive dynamics
* Contracts that may warrant earlier capture attention
* Programs and accounts worth monitoring

The intent is simple:

> **Don't just show me what contracts exist. Help me understand which ones deserve my attention.**

### About the reports

The briefs are experimental decision-support tools generated from publicly available federal procurement information. They are not authoritative statements about future procurements or guaranteed opportunities.

Please independently verify underlying procurement information before making business decisions.
